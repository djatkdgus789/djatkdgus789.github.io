Hello! I am a Ph.D. student (Combined M.S.–Ph.D. course) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the Ulsan National Institute of Science and Technology (UNIST), where I work in the System Software Laboratory with Prof. Young-ri Choi.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering from Myongji University, where I was an undergraduate researcher at the Intelligence Data Processing Laboratory, advised by Prof. Jik-Soo Kim.

I received travel grants for OSDI'23 and SOSP'25, and served as a student volunteer at SOSP'25.

My research interests lie in:

  • Serverless Computing Systems
  • Heterogeneous Memory Systems
  • Efficient AI Systems
